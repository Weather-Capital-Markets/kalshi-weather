Quoting-policy input
Settlement retention is higher on busier days: correlation interval excludes zero, the top decile beats the bottom five under both weightings, and the quietest decile is negative on the trade-weighted measure. A quoting rule that used this pattern would need busy days identified ex ante, or withdrawal on quiet ones. That belongs in Part D's feature set. It is not a fitted policy.

Mid estimators
Corpus headlines use the strict touch mid. The prior YES-print estimator is computed on each fill and covered by unit tests. This report does not aggregate it.
